# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP (ScrollTrigger), Lenis smooth scroll, Three.js

## Users
Enterprise and brand clients evaluating the agency and seeking a clear, deep understanding of who the CEO (Randy) is, his leadership, vision, and team capabilities.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Randy's leadership, vision, and agency capabilities through a premium, scroll-driven web experience that converts client interest into high-value partnerships.

## Positioning
An executive-led creative & technical agency portfolio that combines executive credibility with award-winning creative engineering.

## Operating Context
Web visitors across desktop and mobile devices evaluating agency expertise, reading executive profile & service offerings, and initiating contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- High-performance web experience leveraging Next.js (App Router), GSAP, Lenis, and Three.js.
- Responsive "Split-to-Stack" pinning layouts (pinned 50:50 on desktop, clean stacked layout on mobile).
- Strict mobile/iOS safeguards (dvh sizing, native touch scrolling, matchMedia ScrollTrigger cleanup).

## Brand Commitments
- Personal executive & agency brand centered around CEO Randy.

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing Next.js 15 app structure with GSAP, Lenis, Three.js, and Tailwind CSS configured.

## Product Principles
1. Executive Authority First: Lead with strong leadership presence and clear positioning.
2. Impeccable Craft & Motion: Deliver award-winning micro-interactions and smooth scroll performance.
3. Uncompromised Mobile UX: Ensure desktop pinnings collapse into clean, accessible mobile layouts without scroll hijacking.
